## Values and derivatives

Equal interpolation of normals (1,0,0) and (0,1,0) gives (.5,.5,0), with length sqrt(.5). Renormalizing gives (1/sqrt(2),1/sqrt(2),0). SH with these inputs generally differs. B1 preserves the source's unnormalized interpolated normals and stops their gradients in the fitting composition. Generic gffx normal gradients remain available.

For `y = stop_gradient(x) * a`, at x=2 and a=3, the forward value is 6, dy/da=2 and dy/dx=0 under that contract. Ordinary `x*a` has dy/dx=3. Recomputing stopped fields during finite differences tests a different derivative. Independent local checks preserve declared stopped fields and smooth branches; actual visibility/texel crossings are reported separately. Such local evidence does not prove all boundary derivatives.
